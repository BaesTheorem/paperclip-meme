office assistant meme generator

Type below. The image updates as you type, and downloads as a square PNG.

customize text and options

Lines starting with * become radio buttons, _ becomes a checkbox. Blank lines are spacing.

example

It looks like you are about to make a
horrible life decision.

Would you like help?

* Reconsider my options
* Construct a transparent rationalization
* Learn to live with regret
_ Don't show me this tip again

Local clone of kirk.is/tools/clippy, with PNG export. Mascot art comes from assets/mascot.js (bin/fetch-mascot); the file picker swaps in anything else.
